> A simple flat monochrome Windows app icon: two musical-note stems and circular note heads joined by a single bridge arch. Off-white mark centered inside a near-black rounded square, transparent outside. Thick geometric shapes, generous margins, legible at 16/32 px. No lettering, fine lines, equalizer bars, headphones, gradients, gloss, shadows, 3D or texture.
